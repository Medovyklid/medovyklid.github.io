Technical background

What exactly we build

Fully proprietary construction — modular timber-framed building with straw panels under 40 m², no building permit required (only notification), no building plot needed (ground screws).

ParameterValue
Overall cabin size3.6 × 8.4 m
Built-up / usable area30.24 m² (incl. terrace approx. 45 m²)
ConstructionModular, straw panels (1.2 m module)
Capacity2 people
Energy classA0 (self-sufficient)
Grid connectionNone (off-grid)
Straw bales100 pcs / cabin, thermal resistance R = 4–6 m²K/W
FrameSpruce timber 60×160 mm (posts) + 100×160 mm (ring beam)
RoofGable, trapezoid sheet metal, rafters 60×160 mm
FacadeWooden cladding (spruce), 65 m²
FoundationsFootings or ground screws (8–12 pcs) — no concrete foundations
🏠

Layout 30.24 m²

Bedroom approx. 12 m² (bed 180×200, floor-to-ceiling window, skylight above the bed) · Bathroom+WC+shower approx. 5 m² (24V mist shower) · Living corner/kitchenette approx. 8 m² · Apitherapy room approx. 5 m².

☀️

Energy

PV panels 2 kWp (4× 500 W) + LiFePO4 battery 5–7 kWh + MPPT controller + 3 kW inverter. TEG module 300–600 W from the stove. Consumption only 2.7 kWh/day, production approx. 8 kWh/day.

🔥

Heating & hot water

Wood-burning stove insert 6–7 kW with heat exchanger (3–4 kW), 300 l thermal storage, underfloor heating, 80 l boiler, 24V DC circulation pumps.

💧

Water system

Rainwater from the roof, 1,000 l tank + 1,500–2,000 l pond. 4-stage filtration (root, mechanical, carbon, UV). Consumption only 5–7 m³/year.

🚽

WC & waste

Separating flush toilet without chemicals — urine and solids separated, composting box serviced every 2–4 weeks.

🐝

Apitherapy

4× Flow Hive hives (hassle-free honey extraction) in the room under the lounger. Bee vibration 250–500 Hz, honey and propolis scent — calming effect on the nervous system.

🌬️

Ventilation & climate

Controlled ventilation with heat recovery, DC fans, natural humidity regulation (wood, clay, lime).

🛠️

Service intervals

Cleaning between guests · WC every 2–4 weeks · filters 2× per year · PV and stove 1× per year · beekeeper monthly.

Building regulations: Modular construction on footings does not require a building permit — up to 25 m² of built-up area per § 79 (1) (c) of the Building Act; timber buildings under 40 m² only require notification of construction.
